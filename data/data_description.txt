Dataset: Plant_DTS.xls
Source: Synthetic dataset created for Power BI performance analysis

Columns:
- Product_ID: unique product identifier
- Sales_USD: sales amount in USD
- Quantity: number of units sold
- Price_USD: unit price in USD
- COGS_USD: cost of goods sold
- Date_Time: transaction date

Scope:
- Time period: 2022–2024
- Regions: multiple countries
- Products: 10 product types

Used for:
Power BI dashboards analyzing Sales, Quantity, and Gross Profit (YTD vs PYTD).
